# Worked examples: fact versus staging

These are prompt-writing examples, **not successful generation samples**. Source links support only the listed character/technique facts. Each encounter location and sequence is newly authored. Reopen the pages for a new request; do not assume an example licenses another form, era or ability.

## Dragon Ball: Goku and Vegeta, one visible beam exchange

**Fact ledger.** The [Dragon Ball Official Site's Kamehameha study](https://en.dragon-ball-official.com/news/01_659.html) lists Goku's Kamehameha in response to Vegeta's Galick Gun in manga Tale 231. It supports those two named attacks and their users at that point of the story. It does **not** support the invented quarry, paving slab, camera path or dust palette below. This source is an editorial study that itself notes its tally is not an officially recognized statement of fact; use its narrow explicit manga reference, not its counts as a universal rule.

**English T2VA prompt (approximately 5 seconds, if the chosen workflow supports it):**

```text
integrated_multimodal_description: [Shot 1] Hand-drawn Dragon Ball anime action at the manga Tale 231 stage. Goku stands at screen left and Vegeta at screen right; neither transforms during this shot. A low intact stone paving slab lies on the quarry floor between them, under their open firing lane. A medium-wide side view keeps both hands, the lane, and the slab visible. Vegeta plants his feet and releases one Galick Gun from his hands toward Goku; Goku cups his hands and drives one Kamehameha into its oncoming path. Both beam roots remain attached to their respective emitters. The beams meet at one luminous junction directly over the slab; a narrow downward splash from that same junction strikes its top. Keep both silhouettes, beam roots, and the junction visible through the flash. Only the struck top chips; fragments and dust move outward from that point while both fighters hold position as the light falls. Colored energy glow and hand-drawn speed strokes frame the clash. The two rising energy tones meet in one pulse at the junction, followed by a stone crack.

overall_soundscape: Two rising energy tones converge into one centered pulse at beam contact. A short stone crack and falling grit follow as the light fades.

non_diegetic_music: N/A
```

**中文时序。** 0–1秒锁定左右站位、双手及两人之间的低石板；1–2.2秒贝吉塔的加力克炮与悟空龟派气功各自从施放者双手发出；2.2–3.8秒两束在石板正上方相撞，同一交点的少量能量向下溅到石板；3.8–5秒只有石板受击的顶面碎裂。石板、地形、相持和碎裂结果均是**依据原作能力的原创假想编排**，不是原作事件。此示例专门把“发射者—光束—交点—接触石板—破坏”写清；它尚未经新视频生成证明有效。

## Naruto: Naruto's palm-held Rasengan in a short exchange

**Fact ledger.** The [Naruto Official Site Rasengan entry](https://naruto-official.com/en/news/01_1802) names Naruto as a user and describes a compressed, rotating chakra ball in the palm. An [official Shippuden figure article](https://naruto-official.com/en/news/01_1365) explicitly associates Naruto's Rasengan with that anime period. Neither establishes the invented opponent, courtyard or outcome below. To avoid an unsupported named second character or technique, this example uses an **unnamed adult shinobi** with an ordinary held practice staff.

**English T2VA prompt (approximately 5 seconds, if the chosen workflow supports it):**

```text
integrated_multimodal_description: [Shot 1] Hand-drawn Naruto: Shippuden anime action in an original stone practice court. Naruto stands at screen left with a palm-held rotating Rasengan; one unnamed adult shinobi at screen right holds one wooden practice staff across the body. An intact paving slab lies between them. A wide static side view shows Naruto's palm, the staff, both planted feet, and the slab. Naruto advances in two grounded steps as the defender turns the staff across his approaching forearm. Wood touches the forearm before the Rasengan reaches the paving. Naruto redirects his palm downward and the Rasengan contacts the near edge of the slab; the chakra vortex stays attached to his palm. A bright contact ring and stone chips start at that touch point while the defender recoils away from the same edge. Naruto withdraws his hand. The edge remains chipped as dust settles and both figures remain visible. Blue chakra light stays on the Rasengan; white speed strokes are drawn motion accents. Two footfalls and a wood-on-cloth tap lead into a tight chakra whirr and one stone crack.

overall_soundscape: Two footfalls cross the stone court, followed by wood tapping cloth and a close chakra whirr. One stone crack and falling grit end the exchange.

non_diegetic_music: N/A
```

**中文时序。** 0–1秒给出掌中螺旋丸、木棍和石板边缘；1–2.2秒对方木棍触及鸣人前臂；2.2–3.6秒鸣人改向让掌中查克拉球击中石板近边；3.6–5秒仅该边缘碎裂并保持损伤。官方来源只支持鸣人的螺旋丸形态与使用者；未具名对手、木棍、场地及改向动作均属**原创假想编排**，不指代正史一战。此示例不声称已经生成出合格视频。

## Naruto: a longer occlusion and side exchange

This example extends only the verified Naruto/Rasengan facts above into **original hypothetical staging**. The [official Rasengan entry](https://naruto-official.com/en/news/01_1802) supports Naruto as a wielder and a compressed, rotating chakra ball in the palm; the [official Shippuden figure article](https://naruto-official.com/en/news/01_1365) supports the broad Shippuden association but is merchandise, not a frame-by-frame costume source. The unnamed adult opponent, wooden staff, pillar, hand choice, routes, stone contact, sound and visual treatment are newly authored. No named technique, outfit detail or power is assigned to the opponent. Recheck these pages and any appearance image before adapting this example to a new period or a more detailed costume.

The private drafting ledger makes the intended continuity inspectable; it is **not part of the H3 prompt**:

| Beat | Naruto and Rasengan | Opponent and staff | Fixed landmark and visible result |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Setup, 0–3.2 s | Left of the pillar, chakra ball in his right palm; moves toward its near side | Right of the pillar, staff in his right hand; sweeps once into the open lane | One solid pillar separates the initial sides; no chip yet |
| Crossing, 3.2–7.7 s | Circles along the camera-near side, left to right; disappears briefly behind the pillar's near edge | Circles along the far side, right to left; disappears briefly behind its far edge | Both routes go **around** the same pillar; the actors exchange sides without a cut that invents a new location |
| Resolution, 7.7–12.25 s | Reappears to the pillar's right, still carrying the same palm-held chakra ball; grazes the pillar's right edge after the opponent retreats | Reappears to the left with the same staff; retreats without being hit | Only the pillar's right edge chips; that chip remains visible after the dust clears |

**English T2VA prompt (about 12.25 seconds only if the active workflow supports that duration):**

```text
integrated_multimodal_description: [Shot 1] 2D Naruto: Shippuden anime action in an original stone court. A solid pillar stands at center. Naruto Uzumaki starts left with a compact rotating Rasengan in his right palm; an unnamed adult shinobi starts right with a wooden staff in his right hand. A near-side medium-wide view includes both, the pillar, their hands and the open lane. The shinobi sweeps the staff once; Naruto ducks and advances toward the near face, chakra rotation attached to his palm. Thin ink strokes trace the staff; the Rasengan sends a tight moving glow and flickering reflections over the stone. Wood swishes over Naruto's footfalls.

[Shot 2] At 00:03.200, the camera cuts to a raised view from the same near side. Naruto travels left to right along the pillar's near face; the shinobi travels right to left along its far face. The pillar briefly hides each. Naruto's chakra glow follows his near-side route; the staff stays with the shinobi on the far side. Dust ribbons and drawn motion accents follow both routes without hiding the pillar's edges. Their footsteps pass on opposite sides of the stone.

[Shot 3] At 00:07.700, the camera cuts back to a near-side medium-wide view. Naruto emerges right of the pillar, Rasengan still in his right palm; the same shinobi emerges left, still gripping the staff. The shinobi retreats beyond Naruto's reach. Naruto turns his palm to the pillar's right edge; the rotating chakra briefly touches stone, not the shinobi. A tight contact ring and inked impact rays launch bright chips; dust crosses low in the foreground while both bodies, the palm and the contact point remain readable. That edge stays chipped after the glow fades. Naruto lowers his hand; both settle on their exchanged sides, staff still with the shinobi. One close stone crack follows the chakra whirr; loose grit falls as their steps stop.

overall_soundscape: A wooden staff swishes once above two sets of footfalls on stone. Opposite-side footsteps circle the pillar under a close chakra whirr; one sharp stone crack and falling grit follow the final contact.

non_diegetic_music: Fast hand-drum pulses and low strings build through the circling paths, strike one accented beat at the stone contact, then fall away.
```

**中文时序与核查目标。** 0–3.2 秒左侧鸣人携掌中螺旋丸闪开木棍；3.2–7.7 秒两人沿柱子近、远两侧相向绕行，短暂各被遮挡；7.7–12.25 秒鸣人从右侧再现、对手从左侧再现，木棍仍归对手，螺旋丸仍归鸣人，只让柱子右缘受击并保持缺口。复看时要分别核人物是否凭空复制、是否真的绕柱换侧、遮挡后武器与效果是否跟对人、最后伤痕是否在同一位置。原作来源只证明鸣人与螺旋丸的有限事实；以上换位和石损不是正史事件。此例未经 H3 生成验证，不能当作语义桥有效案例；它也不要求所有新场景使用柱子、石损、固定镜头或 12 秒。
