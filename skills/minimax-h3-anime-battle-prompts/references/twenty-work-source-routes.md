# Twenty narrow anime source routes

Use this index when the user asks for one of the project's 20 anime works and has not supplied a different cast or period. It gives a **starting route**, not a general character database. These leads come from a source audit completed on 2026-09-26; this reference does not claim that the linked pages or images were rechecked today. Reopen the relevant official page, inspect any image before repeating a visual claim, and record the exact fact, medium, period and access limit in the prompt's canon ledger. If a source is inaccessible or no longer supports a detail, omit that detail or find another primary source. Other characters, forms, abilities and game versions require their own evidence.

The cast and action in each row may be used in a **new hypothetical encounter**. Some works have a second, panel-matched route; select one cast and period rather than combining details across rows. The links support only the narrow character or ability facts described; they do not prove that the proposed encounter occurred, that an effect has a particular color, or that H3 will render it well. A work appearing here is not training admission or video validation.
