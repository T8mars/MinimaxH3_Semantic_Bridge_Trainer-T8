# Sources and original staging

Use this reference when a draft mixes costume periods, relies on a different visual medium, or is stalled on one unsupported move. The purpose is a usable, faithful new battle prompt, not a reconstruction of an existing episode.

## Separate the claims

Record the source-backed character, era/form, visible equipment and ability. Then separately describe the hypothetical location, attack order, camera, target shape and short consequence. Telekinesis needs evidence of the ability; a newly designed ordinary stone slab does not need to have appeared in the source. An impact star can be animation treatment; literal fire damage needs an appropriate ability. A character choosing a palm strike in one shot does not imply they lack other weapons or powers.

An unresolved old draft does not block the entire work. Use another verified character pair or a simpler supported action, carrying forward the old draft's unresolved status. A chest-up shot need not invent unseen shoes. Ordinary physical contact with a visible mechanical palm need not depend on proof of a transforming arm blade. Do not turn this flexibility into invented supernatural powers or a canonical victory claim.

## Match reference to the claim

| Reference | What it can support | What still needs checking |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Official character profile text | The abilities or equipment actually named | Whether they coexist in the selected period |
| Official character illustration | Features actually visible in that illustration | Episode-specific costume and hidden details |
| Official episode still and synopsis | Visible frame details and the stated event | Unseen motion, off-frame outfit, and unstated mechanism |
| Official packaging or licensed figure | Its own depiction, with the medium labeled | Whether that detail appears in the selected screen adaptation |

Opening a page is not the same as inspecting its image. Describe a visual detail only after viewing the relevant asset. An inaccessible article's search snippet remains a lead, not completed verification. Keep uncertainty in the ledger and omit unsupported details from the copy-ready prompt.

## Worked source correction: Nami and Ulti

The [official Nami profile](https://one-piece.com/character/nami/index.html) and [Ulti profile](https://one-piece.com/character/ulti/index.html) are useful identity references, but do not automatically define their outfits in a particular episode. The inspected profile illustration shows Nami in a green-and-white striped bikini top and blue jeans. Ulti's inspected full-body profile image shows red shoes and bare lower legs; replacing these with boots is unsupported.

The [episode 1038 synopsis and stills](https://one-piece.com/anime/o6323/index.html) tie the Clima-Tact lightning encounter to Zeus and show a different Wano outfit for Nami. Do not combine that costume with the profile costume as though they were one frame, or transfer a Zeus-specific mechanism to an unspecified earlier weather attack. For a new prompt, choose the period and its evidence, or explicitly use verified profile appearances in a hypothetical encounter compatible with the stated abilities. Limit framing if the available sources support only upper-body details.

These sources support prompt facts, not a generated-video quality claim. If such an error is found after an A/B prompt was frozen, preserve that experiment and report the source defect. A corrected prompt receives a new version and a separate comparison; do not silently replace an unfavorable control or retroactively call the original canon-verified.
